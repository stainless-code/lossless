Add the DeepSeek adapter. `applyLossless` composes lossless memory into one harness composition, opt-in through `enabled`: every session mirrors into its own sidecar, and the returned disposer stops the mirror, the tools, and the command. It registers `lcm_grep`, `lcm_describe`, `lcm_expand`, and `lcm_expand_query`, plus the `/lcm` verbs `status`, `report`, `backup`, `export`, `import`, `gc`, `migrate`, `heal`, `diagnose`, and `compact`. The summarizer engine mounts as the host's compaction backend: automatic pressure compaction stays off until `auto` opts in, manual `/lcm compact` commits through the DAG bracket, and `bracketAuto` routes the automatic path through the bracket too. `lcm_grep` takes `query` or `pattern` with `scope` into past sessions of the same global sidecar directory, where `sessions` and `all_sessions` are equivalent, and `lcm_expand` reads a past session's sidecar read-only through `session`. `lcm_expand_query` runs a bounded in-process reader over `dshReader` and returns findings only. Recall covers grep filters (role, date range, tool name) and anchored expand listings. `/lcm` also grows `dump` with save and removed-row flags, gc session and age selectors, effective-threshold status and diagnose output, a configured heal model fallback, per-sidecar restorable exports, and stores that close on dispose. Model calls stamp the calling session for per-conversation routing, and span-mapping failures record a failed pass row. Sessions already live at mount backfill through the same mirror path, paged and capped. Every seam writes diagnostic rows to the sidecar directory log, and `/lcm report` rolls up the session's rows. `/lcm diagnose` builds a redacted bundle, `backup`, `export`, and `diagnose --save` write owner-only (0600) files, and `/lcm gc` is age-based and dry-run by default. The composition value-imports its `@deepseek-ai/*` host packages, which stay runtime dependencies.
